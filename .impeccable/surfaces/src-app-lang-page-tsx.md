# Surface brief: Pim's site (home, work, project pages, about, lab, contact)

Scope: the whole personal site. Visitor mode: Persuade on home, about and contact; Read on project pages; Experience only on the lab page. Audience (changed by Pim on 2026-10-05): businesses and people who might hire Pim or want to know what he can build. Job: understand in seconds what Pim makes, believe it through real projects, and get in touch. Constraints: bilingual NL (default) and EN, first person, no em or en dashes, dark graphite default theme and light cotton theme, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion. Pim's explicit refusals: no AI chatbot, no custom cursor ring, no single long scroll, no dates, costs or internal metrics, no projects that are not his own work, Claude Code only named in the about page's "Hoe ik werk".

Chosen direction: Gevouwen Schaal (curved crease shell), seed key baf6de14, kept by Pim after the restructure ("de stijl mag blijven").

## Direction contract

THESIS: One line makes the form. A calm, professional site ruled by a single sweeping compass arc; Pim's real projects are presented as clear plates of problem, solution and result. Refuses the dark bento-grid creative-developer portfolio, glass, gradients, neon and gimmicks.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite paper #121211 by night, cotton paper #F2EFE6 by day; one crease ink marks the active element; graphite shading only on curved surfaces. Bodoni Moda italic for display and labels, roman for reading, Fragment Mono only for code and data. Arched-top buttons and cards, circle-arrow buttons, hairline compass arcs, roman plate numerals for projects.

STORY: The visitor reads who Pim is and what he builds, sees three featured projects with real screenshots or mechanism diagrams, understands what he can build for them, and acts by opening a project or the contact page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the crease-disc mark and "Pim", italic tracked page links (Werk, Over mij, Lab, Contact), language and theme. Left: "Pim" in Bodoni Moda italic at display size, the first-person line "Ik bouw software die werk uit handen neemt, zoals ..." whose example folds through real projects, primary arched action "Bekijk mijn werk" and outlined "Neem contact op". Right: the live WebGL shell that bends with the pointer. One compass arc sweeps through the headline. On phones the shell sits below the actions and an arched bottom bar carries the page links.

FORM: Curved crease shell (paper-folds-pleats-deployable-curved-crease-shell), dealt challenger chosen by Pim; seed key baf6de14.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
