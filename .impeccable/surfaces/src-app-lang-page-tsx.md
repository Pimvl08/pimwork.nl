# Surface brief: Pim's site (home and project plates)

Scope: the whole personal site, home route and project detail plates. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: friends, classmates, teachers and curious developers opening a shared link, phone first. Job: see what Pim really builds with code and AI and play with it. Action: open a project plate, try the lab, use the terminal, leave a message. Proof: only Pim's own projects, real numbers from his disk, real screenshots and recordings. Constraints: bilingual NL (default) and EN, no em or en dashes anywhere, dark graphite default theme (user choice 2026-10-04), light cotton theme as full alternative, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion.

Chosen direction: Gevouwen Schaal (curved crease shell), dealt challenger chosen by Pim in the bolder re-roll, steered "kakker stijl, indrukwekkend, netjes en chic". Memorable moment: a real WebGL paper disc that rises into a rigid shell along one curved crease and follows the cursor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One line makes the form. Every surface is ruled by a single sweeping compass arc; Pim's projects are numbered plates, flat material lifted into rigid shape by one crease. Refuses the dark bento-grid creative-developer portfolio, glass, gradients and neon.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite paper #121211 by night, cotton paper #F2EFE6 by day; one crease line (chalk #ECE6DA / charcoal #1C1C1C) is the only ink that marks the live element; graphite shading gradients only on curved surfaces. Bodoni Moda italic as the geometer's inclined serif for display and labels, roman for reading, Fragment Mono only for code, data and the terminal. Arched-top buttons and cards, circle-arrow buttons, hairline compass arcs crossing the layout, plate numerals in italic.

STORY: The visitor learns that Pim builds working things with AI and code, believes it because every plate shows a real screenshot and a measured number, and acts by opening a plate, bending the shell, trying the lab and terminal, and writing a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail of plate dots with the active numeral large in italic. Top: small crease-disc mark and "Pim" in italic, italic small-caps navigation right with language, theme and terminal controls. Left half: "Pim" in Bodoni Moda italic at display size, one interactive intro line beneath, arched primary action "Ontdek" and outlined "Bekijk werk". Right half: the live WebGL shell, cursor bends the crease. One compass arc sweeps from top centre through the headline.

FORM: Curved crease shell (paper-folds-pleats-deployable-curved-crease-shell), position: dealt challenger chosen by the user over the roll leader; seed key baf6de14.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
